Our story

A room made for long, happy meals

Bohoo was built for middle-class families: fresh food, fresh ingredients and fresh music, with a coffee corner for the coffee lovers. The bohemian-style room, full of playful details and dreamcatcher touches, makes an ordinary lunch feel like a small occasion.

Come for breakfast and stay for lunch, or arrive for burgers at dinner. The menu is deliberately international, Italian, French, American, Asian and Arabic, so everyone at the table finds their dish.

  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner, every day
  • Spacious room with large family tables
  • Fresh food, music and coffee at the heart of it

Most praised

The signature

The Crusher Burger

One dish is named by name in Taif's reviews: the Crusher Burger. A Tripadvisor reviewer called it a standout part of the meal, and it is the burger the room orders most when the day turns indulgent.

Order it with a starter to share, the tornado shrimp is the one reviewers mention, and finish with coffee. That is the Bohoo evening, in three moves.
